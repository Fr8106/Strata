== VERDICT (adjudicated 2026-10-08 against the recomputed zone decomposition) ==
On driver 610.57.04 the flags do not matter: Portable|Mapped and Portable-only give an identical
matrix; Mapped only buys cudaHostGetDevicePointer.  The rule for cudaMemcpyAsync HtoD SOURCES:
  [R1|R2]  straddling two registered runs (gap or adjacent)     REFUSED at every size, every page offset
  [R1|GAP] registered -> unregistered gap direction              REFUSED  (asymmetric!)
  [GAP|R2] unregistered gap -> registered direction              ACCEPTED (the engine's non-fixed boundary blob)
  [GAP]    / fully pageable                                    ACCEPTED (U arm, no pressure)
  released-run straddle                                        ACCEPTED (post-release fixed tier)
Engine consequence: <=2 no-alias blobs per layer.  The LAST FIXED blob [bnd-per,bnd) is the [R1|GAP]
direction: its fill copy is REFUSED and bounces through the pinned staging (T2 makes that countable;
before T2 it was the silent arm-B shape).  The FIRST NON-FIXED blob [bnd,bnd+per) is [GAP|R2]: its
copy is ACCEPTED - 64ff01e took away only its alias (device-pointer arithmetic outside a mapping was
the Xid/#267 path, a GPU read, not a copy source).
